Heads-Up Wayfinding Experience

A module inside the race app. Event attendees scan a zone poster, pick where they're going, and follow an arrow on the ground. Works with no signal. Never routes through a paid area. Always one tap from the 2D map.

Positioning without a signal

1
Poster anchors

Each poster tells the phone where the attendee is standing.

2
Between posters

The arrow stays on track as the attendee walks.

3
Re-center

When needed, the module asks the attendee to scan the next poster.

4
Step down

If it can't recover in a few seconds, the 2D map opens with the route already drawn.

The design does not depend on a signal. Arrows bring event attendees within a few meters of the destination.

03 · RISK

Working inside your app

Two things we plan for together

Working with your app team

Plan

The module ships inside your app. Your app team integrates it, tests it, and pushes the store release, so we plan the hand-off around their schedule.

How we plan for it: your app team named in week 1, an early build to them in mid-January, and integration support held in weeks 9–12. In the proposal: their cooperation and release cutoff are a condition of the February 5 date. If the schedule shifts on their side, we still deliver the module and the service, and the release date becomes theirs.

Race day: a campus full of phones

Plan

Tens of thousands of event attendees open the same app in the same hour, so the module is built not to depend on the network or add load to it.

How we plan for it: the map pack downloads before race day from a CDN, the module runs with no connection at all, and usage data waits on the phone until a signal returns. We load-test the map service and usage collection before race week. In the proposal: the race app’s stability is your app team’s; we answer for the Wayfinding module and the map service.
